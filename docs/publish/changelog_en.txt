This version makes several things easier to read and understand.

Stall locations now say "foot traffic ×130%" instead of a bare "×130%", so it no longer looks like prices are higher there. All eight locations actually charge the same to buy and sell; the multiplier only decides how many items sell today, and each item's buying price, selling price and today's demand are shown on its own row.

In random events, "it depends on your reputation" used to be hollow — reputation was invisible, affected nothing, and pleas always succeeded for free. Events now show your current reputation, a plea only works at 50 or above (otherwise you pay the fine), and every reputation change appears in the result line. The real costs and gains of choices like filing an appeal, gambling on a dispute, or taking your parents' call are now spelled out, so you are not surprised by lost health or reputation afterwards.

Two small eyesores are fixed too: a lone closing punctuation mark no longer gets its own line, and the bank deposit row now says "1% daily" to match the loan row.
