This version is about choices that used to go unanswered.

After a random event, the game used to just flip to the next page and say nothing — a 250 fine, four points of health, or four hundred followers all vanished without a word. Every choice now leaves its result on the bottom line of the main panel: cash, health, stamina, reputation and followers, with the exact amount that changed. Losing followers also tells you how many.

"Take your parents' call, they send 2200" had a separate bug: the event could come up again in the same run, and choosing to answer a second time paid nothing while the screen stayed silent. Now it appears at most once per run; if it ever does show up again, it says plainly that the family could not put the money together this time.

Two more actions that used to press OK in silence now confirm themselves: buying stock shows what you bought and what it cost, and expanding storage shows the new capacity. If an event result lands on a day with a more urgent notice — the car being repossessed, for instance — the urgent one keeps the line.
