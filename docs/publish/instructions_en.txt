Fully offline, no Wi-Fi setup needed. Turn it on and play.

1. On the cover, use Up/Down to choose starting cash and press OK. Once you have a run in progress, the first cover item becomes "Continue" — picking it resumes your saved day; picking a new start overwrites the old run.
2. On the main panel, move down to "Go to work" and press OK to choose a trade. Stalls and day labor need no capital; delivery, ride-hailing and livestreaming require equipment first. If cash falls short you are taken to the loan page automatically.
3. Press OK on a trade page to start the shift. When it finishes you return to the main panel and can take a second job the same day. The 24-hour bar under the scene shows hours already spent — the further right it gets, the riskier.
4. When you have earned enough, pick "Clock off" to settle the day's income and expenses, or "Take a day off" to sleep straight into tomorrow. Dozing off is announced in the bottom line and cuts your income.
5. Long-press OK to go back one level. Powering off never loses progress.
